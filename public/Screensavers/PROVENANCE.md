# Screensavers: provenance

Vertical videos for the animated screensaver of the module (see the README): one picture spread over the screen and the 12 keys. Both are fictional scenes: every person is fictional and resembles no real person, no existing character, brand, logo or work is shown. The text and symbols on the posters and signs are invented (a futuristic script that only looks like Latin letters from a distance).
