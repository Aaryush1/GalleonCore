# Code of conduct

GalleonCore welcomes people of every background, identity, ability and level of
experience. Participation should be respectful, inclusive and free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

Listen to other contributors, explain technical disagreements constructively, and
help newcomers. Critique the work rather than the person. Acknowledge mistakes,
consider their impact and respect people's time and boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexual attention, personal attacks,
repeated disruption and publishing private information without consent are not
acceptable. These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, discussions and
other project spaces, and when someone officially represents GalleonCore elsewhere.

## Reporting an incident

Contact Paul Woisard privately at **paulwoisard@gmail.com**. Include the relevant
links or evidence and how you would prefer to be contacted. Do not publish someone
else's personal information in a public issue. Reports will be handled discreetly,
sharing information only as needed to investigate and address the situation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, reject contributions and restrict
participation. They consider severity, impact and repeated behavior when choosing
an appropriate response:

1. A private correction explaining the expected change in behavior.
2. A warning with clear boundaries for further participation.
3. A temporary restriction for a serious incident or repeated violations.
4. A permanent exclusion for severe abuse or persistent violations.

Where practical, maintainers explain their decision and the conditions for returning.
A person affected by a moderation decision can ask for reconsideration through the
same private contact. Immediate action may be needed to protect participants.

This project-specific policy follows the organization of the
[IPTV project's community policy](https://github.com/Bit-Scripts/APPIPTV_Freebox_et_HDHomeRun/blob/main/.github/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
and the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
