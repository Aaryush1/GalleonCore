# Galleon Bosun screenshots

These nine screenshots were regenerated on 24 October 2026 from the
project-built Galleon Bosun 0.2.0 on the author's Linux desktop, rendered
in an isolated X11 display. All show the integrated Widgets and Gaming tabs.
`control.png`, `multimedia.png`, `games.png`, `system.png` and `numpad.png`
show five theme pages from a separate demonstration copy of the author's
profile. Installed applications and their icons vary by system.
`wallpapers.png` shows the bundled wallpaper catalogue; `screensavers.png`
shows only the two bundled videos. No personal wallpaper or video was added.
`widgets.png` shows the layout editor with the owner's widget arrangement;
readings are collected live on the capture host and may be unavailable.
`gaming.png` shows recording and automatic Gaming mode settings on Linux,
without simulating an active game or a confirmed recording state.
The original user configuration was not modified.

The captures document Galleon Bosun; game and application icons appear only
inside its interface. Their marks and icon copyrights remain with their
respective owners. These captures do not grant a license to reuse those icons
separately. The Galleon Bosun interface and the screenshots as documentary
compositions are credited to Paul Woisard in `REUSE.toml`.
