# Galleon Bosun screenshots

These seven screenshots were captured on 21 October 2026 from the project-built
Galleon Bosun on the author's Linux desktop, using its `--screenshot` option.
`control.png`, `multimedia.png`, `games.png`, `system.png` and `numpad.png` are
the five first-run page previews reviewed and approved by the owner. Installed
applications and their icons vary by system. `wallpapers.png` shows the bundled
wallpaper catalogue with an isolated profile; `screensavers.png` shows the two
bundled videos using an isolated demonstration profile. Neither profile uses
the owner's personal wallpaper or pages file.

The captures document Galleon Bosun; game and application icons appear only
inside its interface. Their marks and icon copyrights remain with their
respective owners. These captures do not grant a license to reuse those icons
separately. The Galleon Bosun interface and the screenshots as documentary
compositions are credited to Paul Woisard in `REUSE.toml`.
